Reference: Source: seizure ped.pdf | Page/slot: 4 | Marker: Q2
ซ่อนเฉลยเปิดเฉลยSolution readyA10-0007 answer

Answer

3% NaCl 1-2 mL/kg IV over 20 minutes; if 3% NaCl is not immediately available, begin 0.9% NaCl 20 mL/kg infusion.

Rationale

Na 103 with seizure is severe symptomatic hyponatremia. The full source page includes a reference statement directly below the question: treat the seizing hyponatremic patient with 3% NaCl 1-2 mL/kg over 20 minutes, or begin 0.9% NaCl 20 mL/kg if 3% NaCl is not immediately available. Sodium should be rechecked after the bolus before repeating.
