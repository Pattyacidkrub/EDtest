ซ่อนเฉลยเปิดเฉลยSolution readyA03-0001 answer

Answer

Rationale

Pediatric GCS: eye opening to pain = E2, moaning/incomprehensible vocal response = V2, abnormal flexion/decorticate posture to pain = M3. Total = 2 + 2 + 3 = 7.
