Toxicology Chapter 204

Industrial Toxins

Industrial toxicology is a pattern-and-time-course chapter. The board traps are poor warning properties, delayed pulmonary edema, skin absorption, severe lactic acidosis from cyanide, and rapid collapse from hydrogen sulfide.

Board lens: stabilize and decontaminate first, but do not wait for confirmatory cyanide levels when the clinical pattern is severe. Antidotes are time-sensitive.

Respiratory Industrial Toxins

Tintinalli Table 204-3

Phosgene

Poor warning properties: mild or absent initial irritation, classic hay odor, lower-airway acid injury, and noncardiogenic pulmonary edema delayed up to 24 hours. Rest is mandatory; exertion worsens edema.

Chlorine

Excellent warning properties: green-yellow pungent gas, immediate eye/upper-airway irritation, nausea/vomiting, cough/hoarseness, and pulmonary edema usually within 6 hours after significant exposure. Treat supportively with humidified oxygen and bronchodilators; no prophylactic antibiotics.

Cyanide

Tintinalli Tables 204-4 to 204-7

Severe cyanide poisoning is histotoxic hypoxia: the patient may have normal oxygen saturation while cells cannot use oxygen. Think altered mental status, hyperventilation, hypotension, bradycardia, seizures/coma, cardiovascular collapse, and severe lactic acidosis.

Smoke inhalation clue: lactate >10 mmol/L strongly supports cyanide toxicity in the right clinical context, independent of carbon monoxide level.

Hydrogen Sulfide

Hydrogen sulfide is a mitochondrial toxin encountered in sewers, manure pits, petroleum, and industrial settings. Rotten-egg odor is unreliable because olfactory fatigue/paralysis occurs. Massive exposure can cause collapse after only a few breaths, with delayed pulmonary edema and corneal injury in survivors.

Treatment: remove from source, high-flow oxygen, skin/eye decontamination, and ventilatory/cardiovascular support. Sodium nitrite 300 mg IV over 2-4 minutes may help if given within minutes in severe altered sensorium or impaired cardiovascular function.
