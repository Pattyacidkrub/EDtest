1 Pathway: presentation to disposition
1

Presentation

  • Abdominal or pelvic pain
  • Vaginal bleeding or spotting
  • Shoulder pain
  • Syncope, near syncope, dizziness
2

Initial assessment

  • ABCs, vital signs, shock index
  • Focused menstrual, OB, risk-factor history
  • Pregnancy test in all reproductive-age patients
  • If unstable: IV access, NPO, activate OB/GYN
  • Labs: quantitative β-hCG, CBC, type & screen, CMP; progesterone optional

Unstable / suspected rupture

  • Activate OB/GYN immediately
  • Resuscitate: crystalloid, blood products if needed
  • Prepare for operative management

Stable diagnostic workup

  • Transvaginal US is usually first-line
  • Interpret with quantitative β-hCG
  • Use discriminatory zone carefully
4

Diagnosis & management

IUP confirmed

Routine OB follow-up unless heterotopic risk.

Ectopic confirmed

Treat: surgical or medical, depending on stability and eligibility.

Pregnancy of unknown location

Serial β-hCG and repeat US with strict return precautions.
5

Disposition

  • Document follow-up plan and access
  • Explain rupture symptoms clearly
  • OB/GYN consultation for presumed or confirmed EP
  • Rh-negative: RhIg if indicated
2 Key pearls
  • EP can occur with very low β-hCG.
  • No single lab test confirms or excludes EP.
  • An IUP usually excludes EP, but consider heterotopic pregnancy after IVF or ovulation induction.
  • Stable PUL often needs close follow-up, not immediate intervention.

Risk factors

  • Prior ectopic pregnancy
  • PID, chlamydia, or prior STI
  • Tubal surgery or sterilization
  • IUD in place at conception
  • Infertility treatment or IVF
  • Smoking, age >35, DES exposure in utero

β-hCG dynamics (trend every 48 hours)

Normal IUP Up Rise ≥53% in 48 h; often close to doubling early.
Ectopic / abnormal IUP Flat Rise <53%, plateau, or abnormal slow decline.
Failing pregnancy Down Drop ≥21% in 48 h supports failing pregnancy; slower decline keeps EP on the table.

Serum progesterone (helpful adjunct, not definitive)

Level Interpretation
≤ 5 ng/mL Highly suggestive of nonviable pregnancy: ectopic or failing IUP.
5-25 ng/mL Indeterminate; do not make disposition from this alone.
> 25 ng/mL Consistent with viable IUP, but still not a complete EP exclusion in high-risk patients.

6 Methotrexate therapy checklist
  • Hemodynamically stable.
  • Unruptured ectopic pregnancy.
  • No contraindications to MTX.
  • β-hCG generally <5,000 mIU/mL; higher levels increase failure risk.
  • No fetal cardiac activity.
  • Reliable follow-up and access to emergency care.
  • Normal CBC, renal function, and liver function.
  • Patient understands risks, benefits, and return precautions.

Methotrexate regimen

  • Single-dose: MTX 50 mg/m² IM once.
  • Follow β-hCG on day 4 and day 7.
  • If day 4 to day 7 drop is ≥15%: weekly β-hCG until undetectable.
  • If drop is <15%: repeat dose or surgical management.

Contraindications / poor candidate

  • Hemodynamic instability or suspected rupture
  • Breastfeeding
  • Immunodeficiency
  • Significant hepatic or renal dysfunction
  • Blood dyscrasias or severe anemia
  • Active peptic ulcer disease
  • Active pulmonary disease
  • Inability to comply with follow-up
7 Rh immunoglobulin (Rh-negative)

Give RhIg to Rh-negative patients with ectopic pregnancy or bleeding in early pregnancy.

  • <12 weeks: 50 µg IM; 300 µg acceptable.
  • ≥12 weeks: 300 µg IM.

Return precautions

Return immediately for:

  • Worsening abdominal or pelvic pain
  • Heavy vaginal bleeding
  • Dizziness, weakness, fainting, or syncope
  • Shoulder pain or shortness of breath
  • Fever or feeling acutely worse

Key points to remember

  • Clinical suspicion + β-hCG + TVUS is the cornerstone of diagnosis.
  • Discriminatory zone helps guide next steps; it is not a treatment trigger by itself.
  • Most EPs are detected before rupture when early pregnancy bleeding/pain is worked up correctly.
  • Stable patients with PUL need reliable follow-up and strict return precautions.

Exam memory line

Positive pregnancy test + pain or bleeding + no confirmed IUP = keep ectopic pregnancy active until it is safely excluded.
