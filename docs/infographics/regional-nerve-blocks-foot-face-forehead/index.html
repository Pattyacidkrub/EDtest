ED Use Map

Face / foreheadSupraorbital + supratrochlear for forehead and anterior scalp; infraorbital for lower lid, cheek, nose, upper lip; mental for lower lip and chin.
Foot / ankleFive terminal sensory targets: deep peroneal, posterior tibial, superficial peroneal, sural, and saphenous nerves. Block deep nerves before superficial field blocks.
Before injectionDocument motor, sensory, capillary refill, pulses, and two-point discrimination when relevant. Calculate total anesthetic dose across all sites.
Safety trapLAST can begin with tinnitus, circumoral numbness, agitation, seizure, dysrhythmia, hypotension, or arrest. Stop injection, oxygenate, treat seizures, and use 20% lipid rescue.
